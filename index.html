Projects

Two full-stack applications, built end to end.

01

MERN E-commerce Platform

A store with real payments, safe order fulfillment and an inventory-aware admin dashboard.

  • No overselling: stock is reserved with a single atomic MongoDB update, so two buyers can never take the last unit.
  • Reliable payments: Stripe PaymentIntents plus signature-verified, idempotent webhooks. A payment that arrives after a reservation expires is refunded automatically.
  • Server-side pricing: the client sends only product IDs and quantities; prices always come from the database.
  • Storefront: search, category and price filters, pagination, persistent cart and a multi-step checkout wizard.
  • Admin dashboard: revenue stats, low-stock alerts, product management and order fulfillment flow.

02

Real-Time Collaborative Kanban

A multi-user workspace where boards update live for everyone, with enterprise-style permissions.

  • Live collaboration: Socket.io broadcasts card and column changes instantly, with online presence and multiplayer cursors.
  • Drag and drop: optimistic moves that roll back on error, and cards ordered with fractional positions so each move updates one document.
  • Role-based access control: owner, admin, member and viewer roles, enforced on every API route and mirrored in the UI. Removed members are cut off from live rooms immediately.
  • MongoDB aggregations: workspace analytics in one $facet pipeline: cards by column, priority, assignee workload and overdue work.
  • Notifications: real-time alerts for assignments, invitations and role changes.
